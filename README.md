# Project180

A 180 day run from data engineering into AI engineering, logged in public.

**Day 1:** 1 September 2026 · **Day 180:** 27 February 2027
**Through-line:** local data infrastructure for maternal health, and low-resource NLP
**Following along:** #180ToShow on X, [@elmustyy_](https://x.com/elmustyy_)

## Why this repo exists

Consistency is easier to claim than to prove. This repo is the proof. Every
day I show up, the log gets a line. A skipped log is worse than a thin one,
because the streak is the asset.

## How it works

Three levels, three cadences, deliberately not all daily:

| Level | When | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily log | Every day, end of session | 2 min | Proof I showed up |
| Weekly review | Sundays | 15 min | Catch drift early |
| Block review | Every 30 days | 45 min | Did I ship, and what changed |

## Files

- **[PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md)** — the log itself, the thing worth reading
- **[TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md)** — the weekly skeleton I copy from
